FICTIONAL DEMO CLINIC - Riverbank Example Surgery. All details below are invented for a software demonstration. This is not a real GP practice.

OPENING HOURS
Riverbank Example Surgery is open Monday to Friday from 8:00am to 6:30pm. On Saturday the surgery is open from 9:00am to 12:30pm for pre-booked appointments only. The surgery is closed on Sundays and on UK bank holidays. The telephone lines open at 8:00am on weekdays.

CONTACT DETAILS
The main telephone number is 01632 960 100 (a fictional number). The reception email address is reception@riverbank-example.invalid (fictional). The surgery address is 1 Example Street, Exampleton, EX1 1AA (fictional). Calls are answered in the order they are received.

ACCESSIBILITY
The building has step-free access at the front entrance and an accessible toilet on the ground floor. Two accessible parking bays are available beside the entrance. A hearing loop is installed at the reception desk. Patients can ask for a British Sign Language or spoken-language interpreter by telling reception when they request an appointment, ideally 48 hours in advance. Large-print documents are available on request.

HOW TO REQUEST HUMAN ASSISTANCE
Patients can ask to speak to a member of the reception team at any time by telephone or at the front desk. In this demo, the assistant can create a simulated handoff request for the reception team. Reception staff aim to respond to handoff requests on the same working day.

WHEN TO CONTACT URGENT OR EMERGENCY SERVICES
The surgery does not provide emergency care. In a life-threatening emergency, call 999. For urgent medical help that is not life-threatening, use NHS 111 online or by telephone. Please do not use the reception email address or this chat for emergencies.
