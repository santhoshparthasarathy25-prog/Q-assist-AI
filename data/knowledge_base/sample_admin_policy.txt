FICTIONAL DEMO CLINIC - Riverbank Example Surgery general administrative policies (invented for demonstration).

UPDATING PERSONAL DETAILS
Patients can update their address, telephone number or email address by completing the fictional details-update form at the front desk or online. Changes are usually processed within 5 working days. Staff may ask for photo identification when a change is made in person.

REGISTERING AS A NEW PATIENT
New patients complete a registration form and provide proof of address. The practice aims to confirm registration within 10 working days.

FIT NOTES AND LETTERS
Requests for fit notes or supporting letters should be made through the online request form or at the front desk. Please allow 5 working days for letters. Fees may apply for some letters (fictional policy).

COMPLAINTS
Complaints can be sent in writing to the practice manager at the surgery address. The practice aims to acknowledge complaints within 3 working days.

RECORDS REQUESTS
Patients can request a copy of their own records by written request to the practice manager. The practice aims to respond within one month.

PRIVACY
Staff only ask for the minimum information needed. Please do not share sensitive medical details in this demo chat.
