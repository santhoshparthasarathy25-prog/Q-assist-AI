FICTIONAL DEMO CLINIC - Riverbank Example Surgery prescription policy (invented for demonstration).

REPEAT PRESCRIPTION REQUESTS
Patients on regular medication can request a repeat prescription through the fictional online request form, by handing a written request to the front desk, or through their nominated pharmacy. Telephone requests for repeat prescriptions are not accepted, to reduce errors.

PROCESSING TIME
Please allow 3 working days for repeat prescription requests to be processed. Requests received on Friday after 12:00pm may be processed on the next working day after the weekend. Ask early if you are about to run out.

NOMINATED PHARMACY
Patients can nominate a pharmacy so approved prescriptions are sent there electronically. To change your nominated pharmacy, tell reception or your chosen pharmacy.

MEDICATION REVIEWS
Some repeat prescriptions need a medication review with a clinician before they can be renewed. Reception cannot decide whether a medication is suitable for you or change any dose. For medicine advice speak to a pharmacist or clinician.

IMPORTANT
This assistant only logs prescription enquiries in a simulated workflow. It cannot issue or authorise real prescriptions.
